How Our Workshops Work

All sessions are open to members of every skill level, with camera and audio gear provided on-site.

  1. Register online at least one week prior to the session date.
  2. Attend the Saturday morning studio intensive with working industry mentors.
  3. Collaborate on a short scene to add directly to your production reel.
